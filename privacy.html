Privacy Policy

WhatIf Uploader is used only by its owner. With the owner's permission it stores the TikTok access tokens it needs to upload drafts, encrypted, in the owner's private storage. It does not collect, sell or share data about any other person, uses no analytics and sets no cookies.

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok's settings (Security > Manage app permissions).
